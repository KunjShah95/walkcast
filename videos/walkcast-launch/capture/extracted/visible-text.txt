[p] How it works
[h2] You paste a link and
[span] it does the walking.
[p] Walkcast opens your website in a real browser, signs in, clicks through your pages and records the whole visit as a video file you can share.
[span] The old way
[p] Write the script, record the screen, retake it, record the voice, sync the captions. Then you ship one update, and it is already out of date.
[h3] Paste a link
[p] Give it the address of a site you own or may test, and the sign-in details if the site needs one.
[h3] It signs in and walks the site
[p] A real Chromium browser opens the page, signs in like a first-time user, and visits up to 30 pages, scrolling each one. It never submits a form.
[h3] You get the files
[p] An MP4 of the whole visit, WebVTT captions, a transcript and the page list with HTTP status. A voice-over too, if you asked for one.
[span] One recording leaves you with
[b] walkthrough.mp4

[small] The narration, plain text
[b] pages.json
[small] Every URL and its status
[b] Not a screen recorder
[p] A screen recorder films your screen while you drive it. Walkcast drives its own browser, so there is nothing to perform.
[b] Not a mockup generator
[p] Every frame is your live site loaded in a real browser, with the content your visitors actually see.
[b] Not a video editor
[p] It records one continuous visit and hands it over as it is. There is no timeline to work in.

[span] What is Walkcast?
[p] Walkcast is a free, open-source desktop app (Windows and Linux) that makes a video tour of your website from one link. A real browser signs in, visits up to 30 pages the way a first-time user would (8 by default) and returns the complete recording as an MP4 with WebVTT captions and a transcript. A voice-over is included when you add a voice key. Every caption comes from what the browser actually did.
