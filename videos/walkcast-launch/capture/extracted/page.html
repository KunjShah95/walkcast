01How it works

You paste a link and it does the walking.

Walkcast opens your website in a real browser, signs in, clicks through your pages and records the whole visit as a video file you can share.

The old way

Write the script, record the screen, retake it, record the voice, sync the captions. Then you ship one update, and it is already out of date.

  1. 01

    Paste a link

    Give it the address of a site you own or may test, and the sign-in details if the site needs one.

  2. 02

    It signs in and walks the site

    A real Chromium browser opens the page, signs in like a first-time user, and visits up to 30 pages, scrolling each one. It never submits a form.

  3. 03

    You get the files

    An MP4 of the whole visit, WebVTT captions, a transcript and the page list with HTTP status. A voice-over too, if you asked for one.

One recording leaves you with
  • walkthrough.mp4The whole visit, H.264
  • walkthrough.webmThe same take, VP8
  • captions.vttEvery step, timed
  • transcript.txtThe narration, plain text
  • pages.jsonEvery URL and its status
  • ✕ Not a screen recorder

    A screen recorder films your screen while you drive it. Walkcast drives its own browser, so there is nothing to perform.

  • ✕ Not a mockup generator

    Every frame is your live site loaded in a real browser, with the content your visitors actually see.

  • ✕ Not a video editor

    It records one continuous visit and hands it over as it is. There is no timeline to work in.

03Behind the login

Records the pages only users see. Your password never hits disk.

Walkcast finds the sign-in form, including two-step email-then-password flows, signs in with what you give it, and walks the signed-in site.

    • You confirm the site

      Every recording asks you to say you own the site or may test it. Leave it unticked and there is no recording.

    • A placeholder, not a password

      Traces, captions and files show a placeholder. The narration says a password was entered and never reads it out.

    • No bot-check games

      It never tries to get past a CAPTCHA, bot check or second-factor prompt. It stops and tells you.

    • Looks, never touches

      No form submissions. Sign-out, delete, cancel and unsubscribe links are skipped.

    06Also in the app: Walkcast Lab

    Building a browser AI agent?
    The Lab shows why it failed.

    Most people only need the video recorder above. If you build AI agents that use a browser, the same app has a Lab. When an agent fails a task, it shows the cause and the evidence, and turns the failure into a test you can rerun on the next version. It is in the sidebar under Lab.

    The recorder · for everyone

    A video tour of your site, without a script

    Founders, marketers and PMs paste one link and get the whole visit as an MP4 with captions, a transcript and a voice-over. Pages behind a login are included.

    • One link in, one video out. The browser signs in, walks the site and records the visit.
    • Records behind a login, so demos and onboarding work.
    • The narration is written from what the browser did, so it cannot invent a feature.
    Download free
    The Lab · for AI agent teams

    A failed agent run, turned into a test

    Teams building browser AI agents use the same recorder to turn a failed run into a classified, reproducible regression test. You get a cause, the evidence behind it, and a fixture that holds.

    • A cause, the evidence it points at, and a fixture you can replay.
    • Compare a base agent against a candidate over repeated trials.
    • A cause the rules cannot explain stays marked ‘unexplained’ instead of being guessed.
    Read the Lab docs

    Both halves read the same evidence: what the browser actually did, not what an agent claimed. No LLM judge scores either, and every cause is traceable to the trace.
